Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year to farmer families, paid in 3 instalments of ₹2,000 directly to bank accounts.
Farmers₹6,000/yearAadhaar Linked

  • ₹2,000 every 4 months directly to bank
  • All farmer families with cultivable land eligible

  • eKYC now mandatory for instalment

Housing for All — provides financial assistance to urban poor for construction, purchase or enhancement of houses.
Urban PoorHousing SubsidyEWS/LIG/MIG
Key Benefits
  • Interest subsidy up to ₹2.67 lakh on home loan
  • EWS & LIG categories: up to ₹1.5 lakh grant
  • Slum rehabilitation support
  • Affordable housing in partnership with states

Ministry of Health, Govt of India
World's largest health insurance scheme — provides ₹5 lakh health cover per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
Health Insurance₹5 Lakh CoverFree Treatment
Key Benefits
  • ₹5 lakh free hospital treatment per family
  • 1,500+ hospitals empanelled in Gujarat
  • No premium for beneficiary families
  • Covers 1,949 medical procedures

PM Mudra Yojana
Ministry of Finance, Govt of India
Provides loans up to ₹10 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises for income-generating activities.
Small BusinessLoan up to ₹10LNo Collateral
Key Benefits
  • Shishu: up to ₹50,000 (new businesses)
  • Kishore: ₹50,000 – ₹5 lakh
  • Tarun: ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh
  • No collateral required for Shishu & Kishore

PMJJBY & PMSBY
Ministry of Finance, Govt of India
Two affordable insurance schemes — life cover and accident cover for all bank account holders at very low annual premiums.
Insurance₹2 Lakh CoverLow Premium
Key Benefits
  • PMJJBY: ₹2 lakh life cover @ ₹436/year
  • PMSBY: ₹2 lakh accident cover @ ₹20/year
  • Any bank account holder (18–70 years)
  • Auto-renewed from bank account

PM SVANidhi Yojana

Micro-credit for street vendors to help them restart their livelihoods after COVID-19 disruptions with collateral-free loans.
Street VendorsWorking CapitalNo Collateral
